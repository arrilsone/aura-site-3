The method

A five-stage operating system for paid growth.

Stage 1 decides whether scaling makes sense before you spend. If the answer is Go, we operate the engine through stages 2 to 5.

  1. 01

    Growth Foundation

    2 to 3 weeks · Clear scope

    Unit economics, ICP and a funnel audit, ending in a written decision on whether paid scale is viable.

    What we do

    • Unit economics auditCAC by channel, ARPU, contribution margin, LTV proxy, cohort payback
    • ICP and messaging auditWho actually pays. JTBD mapping. 3 to 5 angles ready to test
    • Funnel teardownImpression to paid retention, with a drop-off map at every step
    • Tracking and attribution reviewGA4, AppsFlyer, server-side events, conversion APIs
    • Team and execution readinessDoes the current team have the capabilities?

    You get

    • Growth Audit deck, 15 to 25 slides
    • North Star Metric and KPI tree
    • Prioritized experiment backlog for 4 to 6 weeks
    • Live dashboard, CEO view, daily refresh
    • Critical capability gaps and team readiness assessment

    Go/No-Gowritten recommendation

  2. 02

    Acquisition Engine

    Channels · Creative · CRO · Media buying

    • 2 to 3 channels, not 7, with a disciplined test plan
    • Creative factory: 5 to 8 angles per cycle
    • Landing page and offer A/B testing
    • Daily media buying with an experiment log
  3. 03

    Analytics & Data Infrastructure

    Tracking · Experiments · Cohorts · Finance

    • Event tracking plan and activation definition
    • Experiment measurement standards
    • Cohort retention dashboards: D1, D7, D30
    • Finance model: LTV, payback, scaling forecast
  4. 04

    Product Growth Loop

    Activation · Retention · Monetization · Pricing

    • Onboarding that shortens time-to-value
    • Retention loops: email, push, in-app, referral
    • Pricing tests, trial length, upsell logic
    • Lightweight customer research, 10 to 15 interviews
  5. 05

    Scale Strategy

    Scaling rules · Geo · Hiring · Investor metrics

    • Scaling playbook: stop/go rules, creative refresh cadence
    • Team architecture: structure, critical roles, hiring roadmap
    • Geo expansion: testing framework, localization, pricing
    • Investor-ready metrics pack for the next round
